Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026.

clipnet is a private tool used only by its owner to manage the owner's own channels (Hustle Six and Neon Six). It has no other users.

Data it accesses

  • YouTube (Google): uploads and schedules videos on the owner's channels, reads their videos' status and analytics.
  • Gmail (Google, read-only): reads notification emails from YouTube and Google in the owner's own inbox to watch for copyright claims and policy warnings. It never sends, deletes or changes email.
  • Facebook and Instagram (Meta): publishes videos to the owner's own Pages and Instagram accounts.
  • TikTok: publishes videos to the owner's own accounts through a posting service.

How data is used and stored

Access tokens and the minimum data needed to run are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Google user data is used only to provide the features above, in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Removal

The owner can revoke access at any time in their Google, Meta or TikTok account settings, which stops all access; locally stored tokens are then deleted.

Contact

Contact the owner through the About page of the Hustle Six or Neon Six YouTube channel.
